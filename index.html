About Omega Classic Collections Limited

Who We Are

Omega Classic Collections Limited is a Zambian registered ICT and retail company based in Nakonde, providing reliable and affordable computer solutions alongside classic clothing and footwear to businesses, schools, government institutions, and individuals in Muchinga Province and across Zambia.

We specialize in the supply, installation, repair, and maintenance of computers and ICT equipment, as well as quality fashion items tailored to our customers' everyday and corporate needs.

Our Mission To bridge the digital gap in Nakonde and Muchinga by providing affordable, quality and sustainable ICT solutions.
